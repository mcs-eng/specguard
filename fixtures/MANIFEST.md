## Planted discrepancies

### D-01 — Section A system voltage mismatch

- **Purpose:** The specification requires a 480V, 3-phase distribution switchboard. The submitted fictional Veylan Arcworks unit declares a 208V, 3-phase system.
- **Spec side:** `asterquay_learning_workshop_specification.pdf`, page **3**.
- **Spec quote:** `Provide a 480V, 3-phase distribution switchboard for service distribution.`
- **Cut-sheet side:** `veylan_arcworks_208v_switchboard.pdf`, page **1**.
- **Cut-sheet quote:** `Nominal system: 208V, 3-phase, 4-wire.`

### D-02 — Section B termination temperature mismatch

- **Purpose:** The specification requires 90 deg C minimum terminations. The fictional Torven cut sheet gives only 158 deg F, which converts to 70 deg C and is below the requirement. The cut sheet states the temperature rating in Fahrenheit only.
- **Spec side:** `asterquay_learning_workshop_specification.pdf`, page **5**.
- **Spec quote:** `Conductor terminations shall be rated 90 deg C minimum.`
- **Cut-sheet side:** `torven_70c_termination_switchboard.pdf`, page **2**.
- **Cut-sheet quote:** `Field conductor termination rating: 158 deg F.`
