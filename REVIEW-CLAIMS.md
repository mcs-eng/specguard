# SpecGuard Phase 6b: claims audit and pre-publication sweep

Date: 2026-08-21. Reviewer: the Phase 6b audit session, working as a skeptical reader of this repository's own documentation. Scope: every sentence in README.md checked against the code, the tests, and the receipts in HANDOFF.md, REVIEW-P3.md, and EVAL.md; a pre-publication sweep of the tracked tree and its history; the Devpost draft (DEVPOST.md) and the shooting script (VIDEO-SCRIPT.md). Production code, tests, fixtures, PLAN.md, and SETUP.md were not modified. Edits were made to README.md, HANDOFF.md (two stripping edits and one de-duplication, itemized below), and the new files DEVPOST.md, VIDEO-SCRIPT.md, LICENSE, and this file.

## Verdict up front

The README's load-bearing claims hold: the verification contract is reproduced verbatim from `specguard/gate.py` (receipt R-05), the eval table is byte-identical to EVAL.md (R-05), the ledger-scope sentences are present, SHA-256 is described as chain-of-custody only in every place it appears, and every item in "What the contract does not claim" names a pinning test that exists (R-06). Seven README statements were stale, incomplete, or wider than their evidence; each is fixed below. Two items need Mason: the post-shoot state of `SPECGUARD_GEMMA_ENDPOINT` (C-13) and a one-line caption on the run page (C-14), which is a template change outside this audit's edit rights.

The tracked tree carries no credential, no key-shaped string, no billing or personal identifier, and no real project, vendor, or client data (R-04, R-06). PLAN.md and SETUP.md are untracked and absent from history (R-03). No commit carries a co-author trailer or a generated-with line (R-07). Commit bodies and the review records name the review tooling as process; see S-06 for the decision.
